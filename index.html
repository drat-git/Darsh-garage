Welcome to Darsh's Garage

Welcome to my personal garage website. This site is about the cars, engines, and driving experiences that interest me.

I am still learning more about cars, so this website is also a place for me to share what I find interesting as I learn.

I am especially interested in cars that combine performance, comfort, and everyday practicality. I also enjoy learning about the technology behind different engines and what makes certain cars fun to drive.

What You'll Find Here

  • Performance cars that catch my attention
  • Luxury cars that balance comfort and speed
  • Reliable daily drivers
  • Interesting engines and powertrains
  • Cars I would like to own or drive someday

My Current Favorite Cars

Lexus LC 500

The Lexus LC 500 is one of my favorite cars because of its unique styling, luxurious interior, and naturally aspirated V8 engine. It is one of the most beautiful looking and sounding cars available in my opinion. A couple things that make the car special are its NA V8 tuned by Yamaha, incredible comfort, and a tachometer that physically moves left and right. It is the ultimate grand touring vehicle.

BMW 540i

The BMW 540i, especially from the 2019 model year and newer, is the car I am currently saving up to buy. It features the famous and powerful B58 engine combined with classic 5 Series comfort and luxury. With tuning potential, a modern cabin, and timeless exterior styling, this car truly combines all of the things I am looking for. The best part is the relatively affordable prices at which used models can be found.

Mazda CX-5

The Mazda CX-5 represents the practical side of my interest in cars. It is the car that I have driven for the past 5 years, and it has never let me down. It combines a modern, refined feel with world class safety features, reliability, and comfort at a bargain price.
